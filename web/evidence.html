Evidence · research synthesis and benchmark Synthetic data

Fresh information matters more than a cleverer optimiser.

Which policy best moves perishable, single-use units around a network under random demand and late data? This report surveys eight research fields, implements one representative method from each that fits, and tests them all on the same held-out random futures against a perfect-information lower bound.

      Literature map

      Eight fields, one problem

      Each field has solved a version of this problem under another name. The pill says what happened to the idea here: implemented and benchmarked, used to shape the design, or deliberately not pursued.

      Operations research · perishable inventory

      Perishability makes the state an age vector and kills closed-form answers, but simple structured rules come close to optimal.

      • Nahmias. Perishable inventory theory: a review. Operations Research 30(4), 1982.Establishes the age-vector state and why exact DP fails beyond tiny cases.Shapes design
      • Haijema, van der Wal, van Dijk. Optimization by dynamic programming and simulation. Computers & OR 34(3), 2007.Solves the MDP on a real case and finds a two-level order-up-to rule (young stock, total stock) is nearly optimal. Evidence that a good rule-based policy can be near the true optimum.Shapes design
      • Beliën & Forcé. Supply chain management of blood products: a literature review. EJOR 217(1), 2012. Osorio, Brailsford & Smith. A structured review of quantitative models. IJPR 53(24), 2015.Domain surveys: transshipment and inventory are the most-studied stages; integrated network models are rarer.Shapes design
      Operations research · lateral transshipment

      Moving stock sideways between same-level locations, either proactively on a schedule or reactively when one runs out.

      • Paterson, Kiesmüller, Teunter & Glazebrook. Inventory models with lateral transhipments: a review. EJOR 210, 2011.The proactive/reactive taxonomy. Every policy here is proactive and periodic.Shapes design
      • Inventory sharing for perishable products. Operations Research 71(5), 2023.Two-site MDP: the optimal transfer direction depends only on which site holds the older oldest unit. About 20% fewer expiries in practice. The basis of the age-aware rescue step.Implemented · π₂
      • Dehghani & Abbasi. An age-based lateral-transshipment policy for perishable items. IJPE 198, 2018.Transfer when the oldest unit crosses an age threshold; tuning the threshold cut cost about 61% in their case. Motivates tuning π₂’s constants.Implemented · π₂ᵗ
      • Dehghani et al. Proactive transshipment: a stochastic programming approach, 2019.Network-wide proactive transfers chosen by stochastic programming beat reactive rules on both shortage and waste.Implemented · SAA
      Stochastic programming

      Replace the unknown future by sampled scenarios, optimise now-decisions against all of them, re-solve as information arrives.

      • Kleywegt, Shapiro & Homem-de-Mello. The sample average approximation method for stochastic discrete optimization. SIAM J. Optim. 12(2), 2002.SAA solutions converge to the true optimum as scenarios grow; gives the statistical basis for the lookahead LP.Implemented · SAA
      • Bumpensanti & Wang. A re-solving heuristic with uniformly bounded loss for network revenue management, 2018/2020.Re-solving a simple LP repeatedly can keep the loss against the optimum bounded by a constant. Supports a rolling re-solve.Implemented · rolling re-solve
      • Rockafellar & Wets. Scenarios and policy aggregation in optimization under uncertainty. Math. of OR 16, 1991.Progressive hedging decomposes large multistage scenario problems by scenario. The route to scale SAA to many nodes.Not needed at this size

      Learn a policy or value function from simulated experience instead of solving the model.

      Control · estimation under delay

      When measurements arrive late, predict the present state from the model before you act.

      • O. J. M. Smith. Closer control of loops with dead time, 1957 (Smith predictor); Kalman filtering.Run the plant model forward over the delay and control on the prediction. The nowcast layer is this idea with a conservative demand quantile.Implemented · nowcast
      • Optimal estimation with sensor delay, 2022.The optimal estimator with delayed measurements reduces to a predictor plus a reduced-order Kalman filter. A route to a probabilistic nowcast.Roadmap
      Quantitative finance · risk

      Rebalancing a portfolio with trading costs is rebalancing stock with transfer costs.

      • Davis & Norman. Portfolio selection with transaction costs. Math. of OR 15(4), 1990; user’s guide, arXiv:1207.7330.Optimal policy: do nothing inside a no-trade band, trade only to its edge. Implemented as a band on periods of cover after the rescue step.Implemented · band
      • Rockafellar & Uryasev. Optimization of conditional value-at-risk. J. of Risk 2(3), 2000.CVaR of shortage adds linearly to the scenario LP. Use it if the goal is to protect the worst days rather than the average.Optional extension
      • Mohajerin Esfahani & Kuhn. Data-driven DRO using the Wasserstein metric. Math. Programming 171, 2018.Optimise against the worst distribution near the observed data, with finite-sample guarantees. Useful once demand rates are estimated from little data.Optional extension

      Learn the demand model while acting, and know how far from optimal you are.

      Organising frame

      Powell’s four policy classes, mapped to the candidates

      PFApolicy function approximation

      A rule with tunable constants: π₁, π₂, tuned π₂ᵗ, band + rescue. Fast and explainable; quality depends on the rule’s structure.

      CFAcost function approximation

      A simplified optimisation with tuned modifications: the terminal penalty θ in the lookahead LP is a CFA term.

      VFAvalue function approximation

      Learn the value of future states: deep RL, approximate DP. Not implemented; the literature shows it matches good heuristics only after heavy tuning.

      DLAdirect lookahead

      Plan explicitly over sampled futures: the SAA lookahead, re-solved each period.

      Method and limits

      How to read these results

      Protocol

      • Same simulator as the dashboard and the Python package (identical random numbers in both).
      • Constants for π₂ᵗ, band, SAA and nowcast are tuned on training seeds only, then frozen.
      • All policies face the same held-out futures (common random numbers), so paired differences remove most of the noise.
      • Perfect-information bound: a full-horizon LP per future, solved with HiGHS. Its LP relaxation keeps it a valid lower bound.

      Limits

      • Demand and supply rates are known to every policy. Real systems must estimate them; that will widen every gap.
      • One-period transfer time, uniform transfer cost, complete network.
      • The bound is loose because perfect foresight is very valuable. A gap of 50% does not mean 50% is recoverable.
      • The lookahead LP needs roughly 0.1 s per decision here; fine per period, but grows with nodes × ages × scenarios.
      • Deep RL was not trained, so “not better” is not shown for it; the literature suggests parity with strong heuristics.
      Next experiments

      Where the remaining gap might be

      • Probabilistic nowcast. Replace the fixed demand quantile with a Kalman-style predicted distribution, and feed it to the lookahead as scenarios.
      • Learned demand. Gamma–Poisson Thompson sampling per node, then feature-weighted scenarios (Bertsimas & Kallus).
      • Tighter bound. Add an information-relaxation penalty to show how much of the gap is real.
      • RL on top of the best rule. Train an agent with π₂ as the reward-shaping potential, or let it tune the band and θ online.
      • Risk. Add CVaR of shortage to the LP if worst-day protection matters more than the average.
